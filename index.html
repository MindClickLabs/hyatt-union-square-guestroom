What changed, category by category

Improved in the final guestroom

  • Surface-area coverage 45% → 65% — the big-area textiles and casegoods came in rated.
  • Fabric 64% → 100% of spend rated and Wallcovering 32% → 100%.
  • Lighting 13% → 93% and Lamps 34% → 95% — the final fixtures and lamps are rated.
  • Casegoods stayed rated (97% of spend, 99% of area) as the line scaled from $28K to $1.03M — the biggest line held up.

Fell short in the final guestroom

  • Spend coverage 58% → 56%, and Leader share of rated spend 16% → 11%.
  • Seating 67% → 59% — Provado's seating is rated, but $83K of Harbour terrace seating is not.
  • OSE 54% → 4% — $293K of TVs, fridges, safes and room accessories, mostly unrated.
  • Drapery and rugs unrated in both — $226K of drapery and $209K of rugs at scale. Artwork ($145K) and Mirrors (41% → 16%) also slipped.

RFP answers we can support

Across the current RFPs, these questions can be answered Yes from Hyatt Union Square's guestroom purchasing data and Mindclick documentation. Answers are drafted below, ready to drop into a response. 7 answerable "Yes"

Energy & Water

Are energy-efficiency systems (lighting, HVAC, DHW) in place to reduce consumption?

Yes. 100% of the guestroom lighting fixtures assessed for LED are LED: entry picture lights, bedside and suite pendants, corridor and entry sconces, and the 11th-floor suite flush mount.

Source: Mindclick

Is an active water-conservation program in place (e.g. low-flow fixtures)?

Yes. The guestroom bath faucets and shower head are specified as low-flow fixtures.

Source: Mindclick

Materials, Packaging & Sourcing

Does the packaging material contain recycled content? / sustainably sourced packaging?

Yes.

In the guestroom renovation:

  • 52% of spend and 56% of products by surface area used packaging with recycled content
  • 56% of spend and 65% of products by surface area used packaging with recycled or sustainably sourced raw materials
Source: Mindclick

Does the property take initiatives to reduce single-use products and packaging?

Yes. 38% of the products by surface area throughout the guestrooms/corridor were shipped in 100% recyclable packaging.

Source: Mindclick

Do you evaluate your supply chain based on ESG criteria?

Yes. FF&E purchasing representing 56% of spend and 65% of surface area in the guestrooms and corridors was evaluated through the Mindclick Sustainability Assessment Program (MSAP):

MSAP covers:

  • materials, toxin avoidance, manufacturing practices, fair labor & human rights, packaging, circularity, contribution to hotel energy efficiency, waste reduction and water conservation, and guest health & wellness
Source: Mindclick

Environmental Programs

Please provide your top five environmental programs actively communicated.

Yes. We actively measure and report on how our purchasing choices impact hotel sustainability, including:

  • Guest Health: better indoor air quality and healthier, toxin-free products
  • Waste: including reduced and recyclable packaging and products with an end-of-use solution
  • Energy efficiency: LED lighting
  • Water conservation: low-flow fixtures
  • Responsible sourcing: fair labor + human rights and environmentally responsible manufacturing
Source: Mindclick

Guest Health

Does the property accommodate guests with chemical sensitivities and/or allergies?

Yes. Guestroom furnishings and finishes representing 49% of spend and 46% of surface area are third-party tested free of VOCs, meet VOC exposure limits, or are inherently free of VOCs:

  • Casegoods & seating: Provado desks, closets, headboards, bedside tables, bed bases, desk chairs and suite furniture.
  • Blackout drapery fabric: Valley Forge Fabrics.
  • Corridor wallcovering: Koroseal.
  • Leather: Moore & Giles, at the suite dining chairs.
  • Mattress: SomniGroup twin XL.

Daltile porcelain tile, the Peerless-AV TV mount and the wood base are inherently free of VOCs, with no VOC-containing adhesives or finishes.

Source: Mindclick (IAQ)
